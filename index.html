What I understood

    Try one
    How it works: Claude looks at your photo and your request, then plans the edits with this app’s tools: sliders, colour ranges, curves, filters, crop and straighten, and AI masks for the subject, background or faces. Nothing changes until you press Apply, and you can tweak or remove any step first. It still can’t add or remove objects.
    How it works: a language parser that runs 100% on this computer, with no cloud and no account. It understands light, color, color ranges (sky, water, greenery, reds…), moods, 76 filters, color grading, crop, straighten, rotate, upscale and enhance. It also copes with harder requests: typos, problems to fix (“it’s too yellow”), exceptions (“everything except the face”), things to leave alone (“don’t make it darker”), eras and film looks (“like a 90s disposable”) and the kind of photo (“this is a food shot”). It can’t add, remove or move objects, and it tells you when you ask for that.
